On this day · Science
Alfred Wegener, born Nov 1, 1880
The German meteorologist who proposed continental drift was born on this day in Berlin. His 1912 idea — the continents were once one landmass, Pangaea, and drifted apart — was rejected in his lifetime and vindicated in the 1960s as the foundation of plate tectonics.

The newts are on the move
With the first rains, California newts walk overland to their breeding ponds and slow streams; the season opens now and runs to March. Their skin carries a potent poison, so the rule is look, never touch — after rain, watch for them on shaded trails near Coyote Creek.
